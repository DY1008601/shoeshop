Finding the right sneaker size is crucial for comfort and performance. Different brands fit differently, and your foot size can change over time. This guide will help you find your perfect fit every time.

## How to Measure Your Feet

You'll need a piece of paper, a pen, and a ruler.

1. **Trace your foot** - Place the paper on a hard floor. Stand with your heel against the wall. Trace the outline of your foot.
2. **Measure length** - Use the ruler to measure from the heel to the longest toe.
3. **Measure width** - Measure the widest part of your foot.
4. **Repeat** - Do both feet. Use the larger measurement.

## Brand-Specific Fit Notes

- **Nike** - Generally true to size. Some performance models run narrow.
- **Adidas** - True to size for most models. Ultraboost runs slightly large.
- **New Balance** - Consistent sizing. Wide widths available.
- **ASICS** - Runs slightly small. Consider sizing up half a size.
- **HOKA** - True to size with a roomy toe box.

## Quick Tips

- Measure your feet in the evening when they're slightly swollen
- Always try shoes with the socks you plan to wear
- Running shoes should have thumb-width space at the toe
- If between sizes, size up for running shoes
