Finding the perfect running shoe can transform your training. After testing dozens of models, here are our top picks for 2024.

## Best Overall: Nike Air Max Pulse

The Air Max Pulse combines iconic style with modern comfort. The breathable mesh upper keeps your feet cool during long runs, while the responsive cushioning absorbs impact on every stride.

**Key features:**
- Air Max cushioning technology
- Breathable mesh upper
- Durable rubber outsole
- Weight: 10.2 oz

**Best for:** Daily training and casual runs.

## Best Cushioning: HOKA Clifton 9

HOKA continues to dominate the max-cushion category. The Clifton 9 is lighter than ever while maintaining the plush ride the brand is known for.

**Key features:**
- Compression-molded EVA midsole
- Early-stage Meta-Rocker
- Engineered mesh upper
- Weight: 8.7 oz

**Best for:** Recovery runs and long distances.

## Best Speed: Saucony Endorphin Speed 4

When you want to push the pace, the Endorphin Speed 4 delivers. The nylon plate and SPEEDROLL technology create a propulsive sensation that makes tempo runs feel effortless.

**Key features:**
- SPEEDROLL technology
- Nylon plate for propulsion
- PWRRUN PB foam
- Weight: 8.1 oz

**Best for:** Race day and speed workouts.

## How to Choose

Consider these factors when choosing your next running shoe:

1. **Foot type** - Know if you have flat, neutral, or high arches
2. **Running surface** - Road, trail, or track
3. **Distance** - Short runs need different shoes than marathons
4. **Fit** - Always leave thumb-width space in the toe box
